Is SEO a self-builder or a self-builder? What should business choose?

For foreign trade enterprises, Google SEO is a long-term effort that involves key word studies, website optimization, content building and data analysis. The enterprise carries out the SEO, which usually has two options: to recruit its own staff to form a team or to entrust a professional service provider to operate on its behalf. The self-builder facilitates internal management, while the SEO service provider can draw on the professional experience of the external team. What is more appropriate needs to be judged in the context of business personnel, budget and website base.

Assess the enterprise ' s own resources first

Before taking a decision, it is suggested that three aspects should be considered:

  1. (a) Web basis: If the site already has a certain content and structure, it can be directly optimized by SEO; In the event of a breakdown of the page structure and more technical problems, it is recommended that a web audit be conducted first.
  2. Staffing: SEO needs keyword research, content operation, technology optimization and data analysis. The availability of relevant personnel in the enterprise and the coordination of operational and technical sectors will affect the efficiency of implementation.
  3. (a) Budget and plans: self-building teams are required to cover recruitment, salary and training costs; The service provider pays according to the content of the service and the cooperation cycle. Enterprises should consider whether they have the capacity to sustain their inputs.

What needs to be done by the self-building SEO team?

The advantage of a self-builder team is that it is easy to communicate and more directly understand products and operations. The main tasks of the day-to-day work include:

  • Keywords research: Analysis of target market search needs, organization of core and long end words, planning of page layout.
  • Web content operations: Continuous refinement of product pages, solutions and industry articles around product characteristics, application scenes and client issues.
  • Technology optimization: Check site speed, mobile end experience, page index, URL structure and internal links, etc.
  • Outside the site: Brand exposure and content resources are expanded through industry media, related websites and social platforms.
  • Data analysis: Keywords, natural flows and query performance are tracked using tools such as Google Search Console and GA4.
  • For smaller enterprises, it is not necessary to have a full team, which could be coordinated by a SEO head, who could coordinate technical, content and operational staff.

What services are usually covered by SEO service providers in foreign trade?

In the absence of a professional SEO in an enterprise, partial work may be considered for commissioning of a service provider. Common services include:

  1. The web site SEO audit: Checks the technical basis, page structure, record-keeping and content quality, organizes problems and optimizes recommendations.
  2. Keywords and competition analysis: Studying the demand for targeted markets and competitors, screening key words for enterprises.
  3. Page optimization: Optimizing Title, Meta Defense, H Label, page content and internal links.
  4. Content development: Planning product pages, solutions and trade articles, improving website content.
  5. Out-of-site content development: promotion of content and external resource development in accordance with industry and market conditions.
  6. Data analysis: Regular tracking of keywords, entries, natural flows and queries, and provision of phase reports.

It needs to be noted that the range of delivery varies from service provider to provider. Business cooperation should be preceded by the identification of specific work content, delivery criteria and mutual responsibilities, avoiding focusing solely on the price of services.

Self-building team and SEOService providerWhat difference does it make?

In addition, in any case chosen, enterprises should retain the authority to manage important accounts such as domain names, web sites backstages, Google Seach Console and GA4 and clarify content copyrights and data attribution.

Businesses can use internal collaboration models with service providers

Self-build and service providers are not entirely competing options, but can also be divided according to the actual situation:

  • (b) Provide product information, industry experience, client needs, review content and feedback on quality of information.
  • SEO service providers are responsible for: website audits, keyword studies, page optimization, content planning and data analysis.

Such a model would enable enterprises to develop their knowledge of products and operations, as well as to draw on the capacity of professional teams to implement the SEO, especially for B2B foreign trade enterprises, which are more specialized in their products.

Common problems of SEO service providers in foreign trade

Q1: Does the enterprise need to arrange for a personal interface after the SEO service provider?

A: Need. The enterprise should arrange for the personnel to provide information on the products, review the content and provide feedback on the operations, and the SEO service provider is responsible for their professional implementation, so that the two parties can work together to bring the optimization closer to the actual operations.

Q2: Is it enough for a firm to recruit a SEO?

A: Depending on the size of the website, the number of products and the complexity of the work. One SEO person can integrate day-to-day work, but other staff may be required to undertake tasks such as technology development and large content production.

Q3: How do you judge whether SEO service providers are effective?

A: Not only the ranking of keywords and the number of articles, but also the inclusion of web sites, the presentation of non-brand keywords, natural flows, focused page performance and the actual Queries.

Operational recommendations

The key to the choice of foreign trade enterprises as self-builders or SEO service providers is to identify what they lack. Enterprises can retain product knowledge, business judgement and website account management, and hand over more specialized website audits, keyword planning, page optimization and content implementation to external teams.

Businesses can first understand the current problems of the website and then determine their own optimization options.
