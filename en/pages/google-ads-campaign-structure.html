How do you split the Google Ads ad series? Planning accounts by budget, objective and operational differences

The Google Ads ad series is not as thin as possible. The question arises first: does this part of the operation really require separate budgetary control, coverage or conversion targets? If the subject matter of the keyword is different, it is usually organized at the advertising group level; If only to look at data from different regions, use the reporting subparagraphs first, without rushing to reproduce the series.

For those enterprises that are preparing to open Google ads or are considering a Google team, the value of the account structure is not “looks professional”, but rather that each cost corresponds to a clear business objective and can be traced to the actual queries.

First, we'll separate the advertising series, the advertising group and the reporting duties.

In the case of search advertising, for example, the advertising series contains the budget and placement settings, advertising and keywords related to the organization of the advertising group. Keywords, scripts and landing pages under a single theme should jointly answer the needs of the same group of clients. It's against Google's official. Description of account level Unanimously.

The search for advertising groups should not be applied directly to all types of advertising. There are different organizational approaches to the Pax series; Prior to planning, the type of series used and the control items available should be identified.

When the budget is limited, don't start with a big account.

Assuming that the total budget available for each day remains the same, the more series the number of visits and queries may actually be available for each series. The result is often that all series are consumed, but it is difficult to judge where it is worth continuing to invest. This is not a fixed minimum budget formula, but a combination of click costs, conversion cycles and acceptable error cost judgements.

The start-up phase can first cover the most important and complete set of needs on the land page. Inclusion of other needs in the list to be tested instead of full first day on line. (a) The budget that must be maintained independently must first be separated; There are no similar target operations at hard borders, and the need for consolidation is then assessed.

Another easy-to-neglect detail: to break into multiple series does not necessarily mean that budgets are segregated from each other. Google Ads also provides a shared budget, which can be redeployed between eligible series; Pax does not support a shared budget. Checks should be made before implementation Scope of application of shared budgets Instead of requiring independent budgetary retention, the series is added to the shared budget.

Example of planning for a B2B account

The following scenarios are used to illustrate decision-making, are not client cases and do not represent a commitment to effectiveness. An industrial pump company sells standard and custom corrosive-resistant pumps, first taking the price of the request for the offer on an English-language website.

  • If the two product lines use the same range of placements and request for quotations and do not need to keep separate budgets, they can first set up two well-themed advertising groups in a search advertising series with links to the respective product pages.
  • If the customized product requires a separate accounting of the cost of the customer and an independent retention budget, it is justified to break the business into another series. A mechanical split cannot be achieved simply because the product name is different.
  • If an additional area is planned for which no local sales support is available, the supply, delivery and response capacity will be identified before any decision is taken on whether or not to place it; It's not a copy of the series, but it's a question of whether you can pick it up.
  • If it is only a matter of knowing which region has a better quality of queries, then the regional report is compared with the sales feedback. Structural change is discussed when there are persistent and operational differences.

Cross-linguistically, take care of the 2026 setup.

The term “a State” cannot simply be equated with “a language”, nor can it be assumed that differences in the language of a case necessarily go undisclosed. Clients should be checked for their understanding of the advertisement and for the ability to read the page and submit their needs after clicking.

According to Google, the language mechanism for the search series has been adjusted since September 2026: removal of the language orientation set for the series and automatic matching based on the advertising language; The search network component of Pax has been adjusted accordingly, and other channels have applied other rules. Please combine the actual account interface and Language orientation statement Check to avoid replicating the ticking steps in the old curriculum.

Multilingual planning is therefore more important in preparing for matching keyword themes, advertising texts, drop-out pages and reception processes. If only English pages were copied to another country without addressing client reading and communication problems, breaking the series itself would not fill those gaps.

Fill out this account plan before you split it.

  1. Operational objectives: What are the results of the ads? The submission of forms, telephone calls and a valid message for recognition of sales should be recorded separately and not all should be considered as a deal.
  2. Budget boundary: Does it have to be maintained separately? Who will approve the adjustment? Is the shared budget used?
  3. Coverage: Which areas do they actually serve? What are the languages of the ads and pages? Who's in charge of responding to the client?
  4. Theme and page: What are the needs and which pages of each set of keywords? Is there a multiplicity of ads that repeat the same subject without any difference?
  5. Measurement: Can conversions be properly recorded and counted over and over again? How does sales feedback go back to the advertising rewinding?
  6. Adjustment conditions: Under what circumstances is expansion, consolidation or suspension? Based on the results of the operation, rather than rebuilding the account in the event of a day of fluctuations.

Avoid losing comparable data when account adjustments are available

Exports the current structure and settings, and records the date, cause and original performance of the adjustment. The phased changes, which do not replace the budget, bids, keywords and landing pages at the same time, have led to a subsequent inability to distinguish the impact. Retain the necessary drop links and check whether there is unnecessary duplication of coverage in the old and new series.

Observation time should cover the transformation delays of the operation itself and be judged in relation to sample volumes. Do not apply generic conclusions such as “must be stable for the next three days” or “replicate certain budgetary security”. Ultimately, a more effective query disk and its quality should be compared, not just the number of hits or the number of conversions recorded backstage.

When you're looking for a Google team, ask for a description of the delivery structure.

In addition to asking “how many series can be built”, it is even more worthwhile to ask why each series is independent, how the budget is controlled, how it responds to operational objectives, and how the weekly reports explain the restructuring.
