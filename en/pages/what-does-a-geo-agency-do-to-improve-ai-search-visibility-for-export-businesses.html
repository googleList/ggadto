GEO · 2026-11-03

What would foreign trade enterprises do to optimize their GEOs, and what would service providers do?

With the rapid spread of the generation AI search engine (e.g. ChatGPT, GoogleAIOverviews, Perplexity), the search habits of overseas buyers are shifting from traditional “keyword queries” to “problems to AI”. When many manufacturing firms and independent foreign trade stand-alone to introduce a professional team, one of the central questions is often asked: What do foreign trade firms usually do when they look for GEO? Unlike traditional SEOs, which are confined to keyword rankings and extra-chain construction, the external trade GEO optimization places greater emphasis on making brands “understanding, trust and offer”.

State of the art diagnosis: an analysis of the technology panel of the website in the AI search environment

The landing of any professional project begins with an objective data and technical examination. Service providers do not just give advice verbally, but do conduct in-depth exercises.

  • Technology and capture diagnostics: the Service Chamber of Commerce examines the state of access to the website, the structure of the page, loading performance, the Robots.txt configuration and related reptile visits, and examines whether there are capture or decomposition barriers to key pages. For different AI platforms, there is also a need to distinguish between reptiles in search of reptiles and model training, and GPTBot, Google-Extended cannot simply be considered as a search capture tool.
  • Brand volume baseline assessment: industry issues such as selection of business products, application scenes and vendor selection, benchmarking testing at target AI platforms, recording of brands mentioned, official web pages cited, and emergence of major competitions, and setting up of cross-references for subsequent optimization.

User problem studies: locking in the core dialogue scene of the AI Search Optimization Service

The user interaction of the AI era took the form of dialogue. Service providers need to restore the true search context of overseas buyers.

  • Buyer intent and challenge scenes: The Service Chamber of Commerce combines business products, target markets and procurement decision-making processes to sort out issues that might be put to AI by overseas buyers, such as product selection, technical parameters, certification requirements, application scenes and vendor selection, and categorizes them as a basis for subsequent content planning.
  • Semantic entity schematics: combing the relationship between the brand, product type, technology parameters, application industries and related standards, and creating brand and product information for the optimization of the content of the follow-up website and for the construction of brand information.

Content planning and re-engineering: knowledge matrix to optimize output depth for the WTO in foreign trade

The content is an important component of the GEO optimization of foreign trade. Service providers need to plan professional content that is clearer, more accurate, reader-friendly and searchable, rather than simply increasing the number of articles or the density of keywords, taking into account the problems of the target clients and the actual business of the enterprise.

  • Knowledge Bank Content Planning and Writing: Rejecting empty marketing software, service providers can plan the content around topics such as product presentations, technical parameters, application scenes, selection guides, common problems and industry solutions. Technical experience, product performance and comparative findings should be based on the true information provided by the enterprise.
  • E-E-A-T expert attributes are enhanced: content can enhance the professionalism and credibility of information by combining the real experience of the company ' s technical staff, product information, project cases and applicable industry standards. External information should be quoted with an indication of the source and should not create a fiction of expert identity or experimental data.

Website structure and technology optimization: enhancing machine readability of website content

In the Optimized Exercise of AI Search, the structure of the website and the page code directly determine the extraction efficiency of the ABI reptile.

  • Full deployment of structured data: The Service Chamber of Commerce prepared and injected the code in person, converted the product page, the company profile, FAQ into standard structured markers to facilitate direct capture of key parameters by the AI model.
  • Pure text and table changes: Based on the current status of the website, service providers can propose an optimisation scheme for page structure, semantic tags and content presentation. For projects requiring changes to codes or templates, the web system, back-office privileges and scope of development should be confirmed and then implemented by personnel with the corresponding technical capacity.

Brand entities optimization: establishment of a network-wide external trust signal and volume matrix

When generating recommended answers, the large model assesses the brand signals and third-party reference rates of the entire network in an integrated manner.

Service providers can plan the content of the network of officials, LinkedIn, YouTube, overseas industry media and professional communities, depending on the characteristics of the target market and channel. The focus is on maintaining consistency in business names, product information, core business and branding. Specific publication of platforms, numbers and means of implementation should be based on the actual scope of cooperation.

  • Eliminating negative and missing information: periodically inspecting brand information in the main open channels of an enterprise, and if outdated or erroneous information is found, prioritize correction by contacting the relevant platform or information publisher and supplementing accurate business information through the official network and other manageable channels.

Monitor the disc: track the recommended results using the GEOservices tool

The GEO effects cannot be seen in a single ranking, but require regular volume aggregations and dynamical adjustments.

  • Multi-model reference tracking and data boards: Service providers can regularly record brand names in platforms such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplixity, official web reference pages, and competitive performance through GEO monitoring tools and fixed hint testing. The monitoring should keep a record of test questions, platforms, dates of testing and responses to facilitate subsequent comparisons.
  • (b) Optimizing strategy and dialogue: Based on the results of the phased monitoring, service providers can further adjust the layout of content selections, page information and branding. For example, the branding under a certain category of issues has been mentioned, but the official web page has not been quoted to check the completeness and content matching of the relevant pages.

Common questions about what foreign trade enterprises do best for their GEOs, and what service providers do in particular.

Q1: Is the GEO service provider only responsible for “optimizing recommendations” or is it responsible for making practical changes?

A: GEO services typically include pre-diagnosis, optimization programmes and follow-up implementation, but the scope of implementation is subject to contractual agreement. Some projects will include page adjustments to the website, Schema deployment, content planning and writing, third-party channel content publication and impact monitoring; Some projects focus on diagnostics and strategic recommendations. The contract should be preceded by a confirmation of what is being done directly by the service provider and what needs to be developed in a cooperative manner or in separate arrangements.

Q2: What is the difference between GEO services and traditional SEO services in terms of specific jobs?

A: The two are synergetic but have different priorities. Traditional SEOs are more concerned with the inclusion of websites in search engines such as Google, the ranking of keywords, natural flows and transformational performance; GEO is further focusing on the comprehensibility, obscurity and potential for citation of brand names, products and professional content in the AI search and question and answer scene.

In terms of specific work, GEO usually places more emphasis on buyer question-scenario studies, semantic entity combing, content planning for procurement issues, brand information consistency management, and on reference and reference monitoring in platforms such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews. The enterprise could use SEO as the basis for technology, content and traffic on the website, and the GEO as an extension of the AI search landscape, which should be promoted in tandem and not as a substitute for each other.
