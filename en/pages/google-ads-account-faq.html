Q1 Can you get someone to open the Google ad?

A： Formal agents could be commissioned to assist Google with opening and account configuration, and it was crucial to use real advertising lead information and to clarify administrator authority, settlement, certification of responsibilities and how to interface. It is recommended that you control your Google account and the necessary account privileges and then authorize the agent. The purchase of other persons ' accounts, the lending of objects or the falsification of information should not be treated as normal opening of accounts; Agent assistance and account trading are different things.

Q2 Do you have to have an independent station to open an account?

A： Registered accounts are different from actual advertising, and the need for a website depends on the form of advertising and the objectives of the operation. Searching for advertisements usually requires a landing page that is accessible and business-like; The corresponding eligibility and privacy requirements are also met when using functions such as the original potential list. P Max, who advertises shopping or uses the commercial Feed, is usually prepared for a suitably qualified Internet store and Mercant Center. The non-shopping type of Pax should not simply be written as having to bind the GMC.

Q3 Can we get a Google commercial on a domestic business license?

A： The operating licence of the Chinese organization is one of the acceptable authentication documents listed by Google, but it does not mean that the licence must be submitted. Account types that are consistent with actual operations should be selected, with the organization ' s name, payment information and submission of documentation, and the delegation of representational documentation should be prepared as required. Different products, industries and target markets may require additional investment requirements.

Official references: China Advertiser Validation Document

Q4 How long does it take to open an account?

A： Account registration, payment certification, advertising owner certification and advertising audit should be viewed separately and no uniform commitment of between one and three working days could be made. Google stated that the state of the latest advertiser ' s certification could take up to five working days to be displayed; Additional information and specific mandates may affect the progress of the process. Check the feedback after submission, by account notification confirming the assignment and the deadline.

Official references: Advertising owner verify status and time

Q6 Do you have to bind the GMC when you open the account?

A： Not necessarily. Only search or video advertising does not normally require the binding of Google Merchant Center (GMC). Shopping advertisements, as well as the Max, which uses Mercant Center ' s Feeds, require corresponding business data and account linkages; Pax can use other configurations for purposes such as getting customers and selling off-line. Don't confuse Google Opens with Commodity Launching.

Official references: Opening of accounts and associated processes in shopping advertisements

Q7 Can one license open more Google ads?

A： Enterprises can manage multiple Google Ads accounts according to real business needs, or they can manage them using the manager ' s account number (MCC). The subject matter of the accounts, the payment and the verification information should be accurate and the creation of accounts should be avoided in order to circumvent audits, suspensions or other restrictions. Whether or not to allow specific configurations is subject to the Platform ' s rules and account conditions; It is not necessary to write “one family, one device, one device” as a mandatory account opening process.

Official references: Management of multiple advertising accounts

Q8 If the account fails to open, can you re-register immediately?

A： Distinguishing between registration errors, payment problems, certification failures and suspension of accounts before looking at specific tips. The information is amended by error, the verification is supplemented as required without passing, and the suspension of the account addresses the issue and follows the appeals process. New accounts cannot be a substitute for complaints, nor should they be circumvented by repeated registrations or IP exchanges. Google stated that the suspension of the related account and the newly created account could also be suspended.

Official references: Accounts suspended and related accounts

Q9 Once the account has been opened, it's a big charge?

A： First, the information, settlement and conversion tracking checks are completed, then the search terms, material and effective transformation quality are judged over time on the basis of an affordable test budget. Budget expansion should be based on objectives, data and terms of payment, rather than on new or old accounts. It is not possible to assert that “a large amount of capital is bound” or “a new account cannot be a P Max”; The selection of the type of advertisement should be based on an assessment of the material, data conversion and operational conditions.
