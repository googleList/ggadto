How do you check the cost of Google's investment? Distinguishing the execution costs of Google Ads advertising, commissioning fees and material tracking, and understanding the scope of the offer, access to the account and the issues to be identified before cooperation.

Google Ads Management Costs at a Glance

Compare the Google quotes with the cost of advertising paid to Google, the costs of managing the service provider, and the material, drop-off page or tracking work that may be agreed on separately. It is not possible to judge the full cost by comparing only the “values” to the “return points”.

Our Google Ads management fee is 10% of ad spend. It covers account setup, campaign setup, ad copy, ongoing optimization and weekly reports. Confirm the billing period, currency and any minimum service fee in writing before work begins. This page does not offer unconfirmed fixed prices or discounts.

How is the 10% management fee calculated?

Management fee = agreed ad spend for the billing period × 10%. Account deposits and balances are not ad spend. Check all three separately when reviewing a quote.

  • If actual ad spend is CNY 10,000 for the period, a 10% fee is CNY 1,000.
  • Ad spend and the management fee alone total CNY 11,000.
  • This is an illustration, not a minimum budget or a fixed package. The written quote will confirm whether creative production, landing page development and conversion tracking implementation are included, and set out taxes, currency, billing period and any minimum fee.

The reconciliation shall reconcile the advertising account, settlement date, currency and back-office consumption; Where refunds, advertising or cross-currency settlements are involved, it is agreed whether or not these items are included in the charge base.

A particular job may also be included in the service fee and cannot be double-counted, depending on the mix of service providers. The scope of the quotations should be clearly set out in the comparison, as requested.

Eight Questions to Ask Before You Sign

  1. Are advertising costs and service costs separately indicated?
  2. What are the rates for services and when are they settled?
  3. What types of advertising and what jobs are the offers covering several markets?
  4. Who will be responsible for the material, the land page and the conversion tracking?
  5. What kind of account privileges are available to clients and how do they view the costs and effects of advertising?
  6. What indicators are included in the report and how often do they communicate?
  7. How are the balances, information and authority dealt with when cooperation is suspended or terminated?
  8. What circumstances require a rebill, and are there other agreed costs?

These are the terms to be confirmed and do not mean that the same arrangements have been made for all items at the station. The quotations were in doubt and a written statement should be obtained before payment was made.

How to Compare Quotes Beyond the Price

Comparing the same business lines: markets, products, materials, land pages, tracking conditions and consistency in reporting requirements. A full-value programme, and a programme that includes a combination and continuous optimization, cannot be judged by the cost of services alone.

Effective information sheets and orders need to be combined with business quality assessments. Platform conversion, chat button clicks and final deal is not the same thing; The “Secure that there is no seal” in the CPC should not be used as evidence of the value of the service.

What to Prepare Before Requesting a Quote

Preparation of a website, type of business, target area, status of existing accounts, scope of advertising budget and desired transformation. When the budget was uncertain, an explanation of the programme assumptions and cost composition could be sought instead of accepting a total price without scope.
