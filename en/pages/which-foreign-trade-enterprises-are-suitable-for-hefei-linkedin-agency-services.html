What foreign trade enterprises are the co-fertilizer LinkedIn's operational services suitable for?

Foreign-trade enterprises that use manure for photovoltaics, car parts or detection equipment often hear the saying: "The customer is on LinkedIn, and has to do it. "and then began to look for a substitute, to get a few offers, which looked the same, but the price was still short. Most of the problem is that the word "supra" is too general. Some are responsible for postings, others are re-organizing the company's home page, others are looking for clients on their own initiative, and the workload, skills and manner of receiving and inspection of three matters are completely different. It would be much easier to judge the suitability of one ' s own, and then to say what services to buy.

First, we'll sort out the home page, content and potential explorers.

LinkedIn is a professional social platform for B2B enterprises to demonstrate professional image, share industry content and establish business linkages. However, it is not a fast-track source, and operations usually need to accumulate over time.

The home page optimization is mainly to improve company presentations, products and services, certification cases and contacts; Content operations include the design and publication of products, industry issues, production sites and client cases; Possibilities development involves targeted companies and job screening, networking, personalized communication and follow-up.

The three jobs are not the same. The business request for quotations is to confirm, on a case-by-case basis, what the service provider is responsible for and whether it includes the operation of the employee's personal account, spontaneous contact and wire transfer, avoiding listening to the LinkedIn whole-case operation without knowing the specific delivery.

Which foreign trade enterprises are suitable for LinkedIn operation?

First, the product needs to have some room for interpretation. For example, industrial equipment, spare parts, materials and tailor-made programmes, buyers usually focus on technical parameters, application landscapes, production capacity and supplier qualifications, and are suitable for building trust through professional content.

Second, the target client is actually active in LinkedIn. If an enterprise is primarily directed at overseas procurement managers, engineers, distributors or branders, it can first look at the target market and client positions before deciding whether the platform is worth input.

Finally, there is a need for cooperation within the enterprise. Product information, cases and certifications need to be provided and technical issues, quotations and follow-up communications in private correspondence need to be taken over by the business team. In the absence of material and follow-up staff, outsourcing alone is difficult to facilitate the transformation of leads.

Content Operations: See if the service provider understands the product

LinkedIn content cannot simply translate the Chinese version of the product into English. Service providers should be able to plan content around product applications, technology characteristics, production processes, authentication information and industry issues.

The same product information can be provided prior to the signing of the contract, and the candidate service provider will be given the opportunity to prepare a test post, focusing on the accuracy of the technical information, the naturality of the expression, the conformity of the content with the concerns of the target purchaser and the availability of clear follow-up. It is also necessary to confirm who provided the material, whether the case was reviewed, and whether there was a charge for the production of pictures and videos.

Subpouse Development: Confirming how the clues cross.

If the programme includes proactive development, the target market, industry, job selection criteria, and connectivity and information reach should be further identified. It is important not only to look at the number of new friends, but also to look more at whether the relevant contacts have been identified and whether effective dialogue has been established.

Cooperation should also be preceded by agreement on the content of the lead, such as the name of the company, the position of the contact person, the information on requirements, the communication records and the current progress, and by a clear indication of who is responsible for responding, offering and following up. The service provider can assist in identifying and reaching potential customers, but it cannot be a substitute for an enterprise to complete a technical response and a marketing transformation.

Impact assessment: not just fans

LinkedIn operates with layers of observation: brand exposure and home page access; (b) Interactive observation, sharing and private correspondence; (a) See links with UTM parameters; The clue is the number of valid conversations, sales receipt and entry offers. Enterprises can check the source of traffic in conjunction with the backstage of the platform and GA4.

The data report should include a link to content, the date of publication, platform data, trail records and stage analysis. The growth of fans can only be used as a reference, not as a direct representation of business opportunities, much less as an equivalent of exposure or new connections to an inquiry board.

Selection of service providers: scope based on actual needs

Prior to the signing of the contract, it was recommended that market analysis, account set-up, content writing and publication, fan interaction, promotion of diversion and data monitoring be included on a line-by-line basis, and that the cost of social media advertising be confirmed, the frequency of reporting, account number privileges and the attribution of material.
