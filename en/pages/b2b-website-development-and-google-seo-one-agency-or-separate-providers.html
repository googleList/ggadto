Can foreign trade construction stations and GoogleSEO find the same service provider? Integration versus separate procurement

In preparing for the construction of independent stations, foreign-trade enterprises often also need to consider subsequent GoogleSEO optimization. The question then arises: should foreign trade construction stations and SEOs be handed over to the same service provider, or should the construction company and SEO service providers be approached? Both models of cooperation have their own applicable scenarios. Integrated services focus more on synergies between reconstruction stations and optimization, and separate procurement allows enterprises to select professional teams based on different needs. This paper will help enterprises to determine which model is more appropriate for their own circumstances by comparing project collaboration, professional competencies, costs and allocation of responsibilities.

Core responsibilities and intersection of the two services

To find out if you want to find the same family, first of all, you have to clear up the difference between the construction station and the work of SEO:

  • Technical responsibilities for foreign trade construction stations: The main responsibility is to provide independent stations for sustainable operation of enterprises by means of web site planning, page design, front-end and back-office development, server configuration, mobile-end adaptation, and the construction of functions such as product displays, questionnaires, etc.
  • GoogleSEO ' s operational responsibilities: The main focus is on keyword research, website technical audits, page optimization, content planning, inner and extra-chain construction, and traffic data analysis, helping the website to raise the natural exposure of the search engine and capture potential business-related clients.
  • The intersection of the two: the construction of the station and the SEO are not entirely independent. The structure of the website, the planning of URLs, the speed of page loading, the title label and the layout of content all affect subsequent optimization. If the building phase does not take into account the requirements of the SEO base, the web line may require a re-engineering of the page structure and technical set-up to increase communication and development costs.

Integrated cooperation: better synergy between station-building and SEO

The most central benefit of the one-stop service chosen for the build-up + SEO is the efficient and seamless interface of project collaboration.

  • Communication costs are extremely low and responsibility is not overloaded: if there are independent build-up stations and SEO teams within the service provider, large-scale technical adjustments can be made at the beginning of the project to jointly plan the structure of the website, product classification, URLs and page content, and to reduce the number of site deliveries.
  • The construction phase takes into account the SEO base: in the development of the website, the basic requirements of the SEO, such as page titles, H labels, URL structures, photo-Alt properties, inner-chain and mobile-end experiences, can be considered in tandem to reduce subsequent duplication.
  • The basic configuration is smoother: GoogleSearchConsole validation, Sitemap submission and GA4 data configuration can be synchronized with the web line in preparation for subsequent SEO operations and website data analysis. These configurations do not, however, imply that the pages will always be entered quickly, and the actual intake will still depend on the quality of the pages, access, etc.

Separate procurement: selection of professional teams based on needs

Since integration is not the case, why do many companies choose to purchase their foreign trade stations separately from SEOs?

  • The vertical field is different in its specialization: the focus of the capacities of different service providers may vary. Some foreign trade station companies are better at web design, functional development and system customization, while some SEO service providers are more focused on keyword research, content operations, site and off-site optimization and data analysis. Separate procurements can select teams based on the actual needs of the enterprise, but the work boundary between the parties needs to be clarified in advance.
  • (c) Facilitating professional acceptance and acceptance: the enterprise can involve SEO teams in the process of receiving and inspection of the website in advance, making proposals for changes in the page structure, URL planning, mobile end-of-way experience, basic SEO settings, etc., and reducing the barriers to the optimization of the web line.
  • Costs and responsibilities need to be clear in advance: separate procurement does not necessarily mean higher costs, but enterprises need to consider communication, coordination and additional development costs between the two service providers. If SEO optimization involves a structural or code adjustment of the website, it will also need to be clear in advance who will implement it and whether there will be a separate fee.

Technical collaboration and difficulty of implementation: hidden challenges to separate procurement

If an enterprise decides to adopt a separate procurement model, the technical collaboration capacity of the team needs to be assessed in advance:

  • Communication and implementation costs: After the SEO team has identified the need for technical modifications, the construction company needs to assess the development difficulties and workload. If the parties do not agree in advance on the scope of the maintenance and the cost of the modifications, there may be cases where the optimisation programme cannot be implemented in a timely manner, or where additional development costs will be required.
  • Website maintenance and modification risks: When the SEO optimization involves code, plugin or website restructuring, the original system architecture should be known and tested after backup, so as to avoid unassessed direct modifications affecting the proper functioning of the website.

Website delivery and authority management: clear attribution of assets and boundaries of cooperation

Regardless of the type of cooperation chosen, website delivery and access control is the bottom line that enterprises must control:

1. Attribution of core competencies

  • Domain names and servers: It is recommended that the enterprise own or own the regulatory authority to ensure that the subsequent renewal, relocation and maintenance can be done on its own.
  • Web back-office and data accounts: Enterprises should retain the necessary administrator privileges to independently access the web back-office, GoogleSearchConsole and GA4 accounts.

2. Clear delivery

  • Website deliverables: The contract should specify the range and attribution of the delivery of the website ' s source code, database, design documents and related accounts, and provide for follow-up maintenance, secondary development and website migration support to avoid a power or delivery dispute when a service provider is replaced.

If open source systems such as WordPress are used, the mandate and range of delivery of the subject, plugins and customized development components should also be confirmed in advance, and it cannot simply be assumed that the use of open source systems represents the free movement of all components.

How can foreign trade enterprises choose to build a model of cooperation with SEOs?

In order to facilitate rapid decision-making, you can refer to the following options:

Priority is given to [integrated service providers]:

  • Desiring to harmonize the planning of the site with SEO from the early stages of the project, and to reduce the cost of communication between the different teams;
  • Lack of internal technical and SEO managers and the need for continuous build and optimization support from service providers;
  • It is hoped that the web site will be delivered with a direct connection to the keyword layout, page optimization and content operation.

Prioritization of [separated procurement]:

  • (a) There is a stable independent station, with only professional SEO service providers responsible for keywords, content and internal and external optimization;
  • The website has complex customized development needs, which require dedicated technical teams;
  • There are project managers within the enterprise who can coordinate the work between the construction company and the SEO service provider.

Common questions about whether foreign trade construction stations and GoogleSEO can be handed over to the same service provider

Q1: What happens when the station is finished and the SEO is not working?

A: Station delivery and SEO operation are at different stages and cannot be equated with natural flow growth. Cooperation should be preceded by clear site delivery standards and SEO service content, such as website structure, basic technology configuration, keyword planning, page optimization and phased data reporting. Follow-up assessment of progress optimized in the context of the SearchConsole, GA4 and PACT data.

Q2: Is it true that many of the construction companies promote "SEO gift"?

A: It is necessary to look at the specific services that are included. Some of the construction companies provide free SEOs primarily for basic settings, such as page TDK, Site Map and base label configuration, which do not necessarily include keyword research, page optimization, outer-chain construction and continuous data analysis. Before contracting, enterprises should identify the specific range of services and avoid misconstrued basic technology configuration as complete SEO operations.

Q3: Is it easier to move a website if integrated service providers are selected and they are not used?

A: The ease of migration depends largely on the system used for the website, the source delivery range and server management privileges. The use of open-source systems such as WordPress is usually easier to maintain and migrate, but the authorization and delivery of the subject matter, plugins, databases and customized development components still need to be identified. It was suggested that the relevant provisions should be clarified at the time of signing.
