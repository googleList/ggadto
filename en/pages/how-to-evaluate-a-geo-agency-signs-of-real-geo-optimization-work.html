GEO Services Assessment: How can it be judged that the service provider is actually optimizing?

The contract was signed for several months, and the monthly newspaper wrote “Access to content optimization”, “A significant increase in the exposure of AI”, but it was tested by itself, and the AI response still did not contain a brand name. What should companies see at this point? GEO is not purchasing a fixed ranking, and AI responses are influenced by such factors as platform, model, search content and question-and-answer methods. Therefore, the assessment service provider cannot look at only one screenshot or a summary report, but rather check the situation before optimization, actual implementation records, testing process and stage results.

Look at the baseline records before optimization.

Without pre-optimization data, it is difficult to judge subsequent changes. In the early stages of the cooperation, it was recommended that the parties identify a set of fixed test questions and record the original text of the questions, the testing platform, the date, the full answer, the reference to the brand name and the reference to the official ' s web page.

At the same time, the website ' s focus pages, content structure, page accessibility, and base information on brands in open channels are maintained.

Note that the page is regularly accessed, which does not mean that it will always be retrieved or quoted by AI. The same questions and settings should be used as much as possible to ensure that the data are comparable.

Reconcile content updates and technical change records

“Twelfth Optimization of the 12 pages this month” is a summary of the work and cannot prove what was actually done. A complete GEO optimization report should include, to the extent possible, the URL of the page, the date of change, the pre- and post-adjustment content and the reasons for the optimization.

Content updates could include additional product parameters, authentication information, application scenarios and real cases, improvement of FAQ, and restructuring of the page to make key information clearer.

At the site level, page accessibility, capture settings, site maps, semantic structure and structured data are checked. If the service contains off-site content or multiple channels of exposure, a platform, content links and the date of publication should also be provided.

Use fixed questions for AI testing

The Transcript Test Set can be understood as a set of topics for periodic re-examination. The questions should be from the search and procurement needs of real clients, and it is suggested that three categories should be covered:

  1. Questions of type: What should be the choice of a particular type of equipment?
  2. The problem of the scene: what are the solutions for a certain production scene?
  3. Comparative questions: How should different suppliers or programmes be compared?

Each test records the original language, platform, date, language or area, answer content, screenshot and reference links. Target platforms such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity can be selected for testing.

The answer to the same question may be different at different times. Priority issues can be tested several times to see how often brands appear and pages are quoted, and not to judge their optimal effects on the basis of one result alone.

Monitor brand references and page references, respectively

It is recommended that the name references and official web references be recorded separately. Brands refer to concerns about whether business or product names appear, are accurately described and appear in parallel with competitions. AI citation monitoring focuses on which URLs are cited, whether they come from the corporate network, and what theme pages should be used.

The reference to branding does not necessarily represent that the official network is quoted, nor does the reference to the official web page mean that AI will explicitly recommend it. The two indicators should be counted separately.

If an error in the brand information or product parameters described by AI is found, it should be recorded in a timely manner and the information of the network of inspectors and other open sources should be accurate.

Quality of implementation judged through phase reports

A verifiable GEO optimization report with five categories of recommendations:

  1. Pre-optimization baseline: test issues, platforms, dates and initial results.
  2. Content and technical changes: related to pages, changes to records and reasons for adjustments.
  3. Original test record: original question(s), answer(s), screenshot(s) and test conditions.
  4. Reference and citation data: brand appearance, citation URL and statistical calibration.
  5. Follow-up optimization plan: current problems and next phase of reorientation.

It is recommended that performance records and test changes be viewed on a monthly basis, followed by a comprehensive re-disbursement in stages.

AI responses are subject to model updates, search sources and question environmental impacts, and fluctuations in data do not mean that service providers must not be employed; Similarly, the short-term appearance of several brand references does not directly prove that the optimization has stabilized.

The enterprise needs to assess “whether or not to perform the work as agreed” separately from “whether or not to produce a phased change”.

Common issues in the evaluation of GEO services

Q1: Is it enough that the service provider only provides an AI response screenshot?

A: Not enough. The screenshot should be accompanied by the original question(s), the platform, the date of the test and the link to the reference, preferably by a double-checking using the test questions identified by both parties.

Q2: Does AI suddenly stop mentioning brand names, which means that optimization is not working?

A: Not based on one test alone. Any changes in test conditions and platforms should be checked, and a combination of brand reference frequency and reference page changes should be analysed over time.

Q3: How often do the GEO Optimization Report take place?

A: A monthly reconciliation of work records and test data can be performed, followed by a comprehensive re-entry on a quarterly or agreed basis. The focus is on having clear calibres and raw records for each data.

Operational recommendations

The real optimization of GEO service providers is to be judged, with emphasis on traceability, retroactivity of data, and whether the report describes the problem and the way forward.

The GEO service, which is hosted by Anhui, is structured around AI search opportunities analysis, AI content strategy, web semantic structure, content value enhancement, branding, multi-channel AI exposure and impact monitoring. The specific scope of implementation should be confirmed prior to the enterprise ' s cooperation, and the service provider should be requested to provide the corresponding pages, content and monitoring records in the process. GEO Optimization does not ensure that brands appear in each AI response. Compared to the single screenshot, it is even more important to note whether the brand visibility and AI reference page share show positive changes in the ongoing testing.
