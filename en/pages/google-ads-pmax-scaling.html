My scalding idea: keep the original series and synchronize the new series.

In response to the "Pax amplification, is it a budget increase or a new series of advertisements? The answer to this question, the author of the contribution, is to create a new series, slightly alter the audience signals and synchronize the tests.

At the heart of this approach is to try to keep the well-run series and place new explorations in separate tests. But this is an individual experience and not a standard answer for all accounts; The new series also requires budgets, data and learning time.

Why don't I prefer to increase the budget at random?

In the author ' s placement experience, Pax sometimes shows a high degree of volatility. As a result, the series of billings has been stabilized and no random changes to the budget, bid, etc. have been made. The authors also acknowledge that some of the series of budget increases can still stabilize.

It is important to add that it is not possible to conclude that Pax is “recognizedly vulnerable” to collapse, nor to deduce that the budget additions have a certain destructive effect. Changes in effects may also result from delay in conversion, tracking, material, market demand or other set-up changes.

For example, an average budget of $50 per day and close to $100 per day does not mean that the budget does not constitute a constraint. Google uses the average daily budget for most of the advertising series, with the single-day spending cap usually twice the average daily budget and 30.4 times the monthly rate; A different rule would be required, for example, for budgetary changes. The daily budget is not a fixed daily consumption.

Method I: New test assumptions from existing signals

Use the signal setup of the existing transformation series to organize common features: model, use of scenes, colour, purchase intent, brand or long end theme. Categorize, then explore the relevant subject, so that differences cannot be explained after the test has ended.

It is also possible to collate the URLs, interests and side data in the self-defined breakdown, and to record which combinations appear at the same time as the better overall results. Keywords and URLs here are signal input, are not the same as search key words orientation for advertising, and cannot be determined by a series of results that a particular input brings a separate order.

The audience signal is a recommendation to Google AI, not a hard-directed border. Pax may be able to show people outside the signal, so a slight change of signal does not represent a new and fully independent audience.

Method II: Split and regroup signals

The author suggests that the already tested signals be split and regrouped. For example, three thematic clusters and two types of interest could form six combinations to be tested.

This is only an example of an experimental design and does not mean that six series have to be created at the same time. (a) Data are dispersed when there are insufficient budget and conversion samples; It is desirable that there be clear assumptions and key variables for each round, so that it is not possible to distinguish between signals, commodities or materials.

Methodology III: Explore the combination of commodity feed and material

First, we look at a series of reports on commodities, materials and channels that already exist, and look for directions that deserve further validation. The author suggests that SKU or material that is performing better be collated separately and explore the settings commonly known as “feed_only” and “ads_only”.

These two statements are popularly known as "Google " , not the type of official independent advertising series; The material and the goods are not mutually unfavourable two attribution pathways. Sets such as Feed, auto-create material and end-to-end site settings can affect actual demonstration and cannot commit to “shopping only” or “ running only some material”.

The current account support set-up should be checked against material requirements to be tested with clear commodity grouping, drop-out pages and creative assumptions, rather than simply removing the material or feed and finding that a channel separation has been achieved.

Method four: Direct copying, really safer?

The author ' s personal test is that it is more secure to replicate a series that is performing well, and that it is better to convert than the direct budget. This experience is retained here, but there is no reversible account data to support the general effect.

Reproduction does not reproduce the accumulated learning results of the original series, nor does it automatically create new demands. A highly similar series may cover the same commodities and flows, making it more difficult for data to be dispersed and for volume to be judged.

Google Retailing Prox best practices suggest merging structures where appropriate to help with learning and optimization. Thus, identical replication is more appropriate as a presumption to be verified than as a default scaling strategy.

Budget increases or new ones: as judged by the overall results of the accounts

If only the same market and the same commodity expands its inputs under the same objective, it can assess whether there is room for expansion of the original series; The new series may be more manageable if different commodity, market or business objectives are to be tested. Either way, there is no guarantee that ROAS will necessarily be promoted.

  1. The baseline is first recorded: cost, conversion volume, conversion value, CPC, ROAS and real order quality.
  2. Clear testing of differences and input boundaries, with no simultaneous change in all settings.
  3. Combined with the conversion cycle and sample volume observations, avoid judging on the basis of one or two days of results.
  4. Look at the new orders and values for the account as a whole, rather than just the surface of the copy series ROAS.
  5. (a) Document conclusions; Re-evaluate, in a timely manner, the duplicate structure, which has no clear value.
