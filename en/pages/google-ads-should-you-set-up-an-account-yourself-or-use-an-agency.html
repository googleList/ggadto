Should Google open an account with an agent or should it register itself? Comprehensive comparison and selection guide

The opening of accounts is the basis for the work to be done before advertising is launched for enterprises that are preparing to develop overseas clients through Google Ads. In practice, however, companies often encounter the problem of whether Google advertising should be opened on its own or with the assistance of a professional agent. It needs to be made clear that the assistance of the self-opening and agent in opening the account does not mean that there are two completely different advertising accounts. The enterprise can create its own Google Ads accounts, or complete the construction of the accounts under the supervision of an agent, and then decide who is responsible for advertising and optimization, depending on the team ' s capacity.

Core preparation for Google ads.

Regardless of which path to open, before the Google commercial is officially opened, the enterprise needs to complete the following basic preparations:

  • (c) Compliance stand-alone or land page: Ensuring that the website has a complete privacy policy, contact details, a return-for-goods clause, etc., which meets the requirements of Google advertising policy.
  • Enterprise information and validation: advance preparation of business name, business address, website information and related business documentation. If Google Ads requests an identification of the advertiser or an industry-specific check, the corresponding information should be submitted in accordance with the back-office advice. The specifics are based on the actual requirements of the account.
  • Enterprise-specific mailboxes: It is recommended to use enterprise domain mailboxes (e.g., marketing@yourdomain.com) not only to upgrade brand specialization but also to facilitate the harmonization of account privileges.

Self-registered Google Ads accounts: process and attention

Self-registration is a simple and direct option for start-ups with a relatively limited marketing budget and a team with some experience in overseas digital marketing.

1. Registration steps for dismantling

  • Login platform: Access to the Google Ads official home page, login using the company Google account.
  • Select the account mode: Select the appropriate account setting according to actual operational needs. If you want to set up an advertising series, keywords, bids and transformation tracking, you can choose an expert model and get a more complete advertising management function.
  • Billing information: Fill in payment information, billing address, currency and time zone, depending on the actual area of registration of the account and the business. The changes made upon completion of the partial account set-up are limited, in particular the clearing currency and time zone, which should be carefully confirmed before registration.

2. Several issues that self-opening can easily ignore

  • Account audit and policy risk: Google Ads examines account information, payment information, advertising content and target web pages accordingly. The lack of necessary information on the enterprise ' s website, the non-compliance of advertising content with policy, or the unusual nature of payment information may affect the audit of advertisements and the proper use of accounts. Therefore, before opening an account, the website and the content of the advertisement should be checked for compliance with the requirements.
  • Consistency of payment information: When filling out the information on the payee, the address of the bill and the manner of payment, ensure that the information is true, accurate and meets Google ' s requirements for the corresponding payment information. Do not use payment methods of unknown origin, or simply fill out information that does not correspond to the actual payee, so as not to create problems of payment validation or account review.

What kind of opening and placement support can Google Advertising Agent provide?

The core value of opening an account through Google Advertising Agent is to reduce compliance risks and to obtain full-scale account support services.

  • Opening guidance and information reconciliation: Experienced Google advertising agents can assist businesses in combing account openings, checking account infrastructure and guiding the completion of account creation and related validation processes. However, the agent could not circumvent the Google official audit or guarantee that the account would pass.
  • Policy advice and assistance on account issues: When the account is confronted with issues such as advertising denials, payment certification or policy review, agents can assist the firm in quelling the reasons, sorting out complaints and providing recommendations for handling them in the light of the actual situation. The final review and the processing cycle remain to be determined by Google.
  • Advertising and data optimization: If the firm does not have a mature advertising team, it may also choose to provide follow-up delivery services by agents, including keyword research, account structure, advertising material production, advertising, data analysis and optimization. For B2B foreign trade enterprises, attention should also be paid to data tracking configurations such as GA4 and GTM and continuous analysis of advertising hits, website visits and conversion of queries.

The Google Ads service, which brings together the Emblem, is not limited to account construction, but also includes Google keyword analysis and research, advertising material production, account set-up and improvement, advertising and regular data analysis and feedback. Depending on their team capacity, enterprises can choose the appropriate advertising support.

Google Ads Account Competence: How does an enterprise retain control over the account?

In choosing how to open an account, many companies are most concerned about the attribution of account rights.

1. Classification and control of competences

Google Ads ' account management involves two different mechanisms for user access and for the association of manager accounts. User access to determine who can log in, view the accounts or manage the ad; The manager ' s account association facilitates the central management of multiple advertising accounts by agents or the operation team. The two are not equal to account ownership.

2. Differences in authority for self-opening vs. agent to assist in opening accounts

What difference does Google make between the cost of opening an account and the way it is paid?

Enterprise self-opening: Based on the billing country and the payment setting, advertising costs can be paid using the Google-supported payment method. Specific support for credit cards, bank accounts or other means of payment will require physical representation at the back of the account. Enterprises should also consider cross-border payment of fees and exchange rate costs.

  • Agency assistance: Some of the agents may provide public clearing, capitalization and related financial voucher services in accordance with their own cooperation modalities. However, there are differences in the way in which the various agents settle, the issuers and the cost structure, and enterprises should confirm in advance how advertising and service charges are charged, where funds flow and where invoices are issued.
  • Policy advice: Specific account eligibility, support payment modalities and the latest advertising audit policy are subject to the latest provisions published by Google.

It should be noted that the way in which accounts are opened does not directly determine the advertising fee for Google Ads. Enterprises should account separately for costs such as advertising consumption, agency service charges, fees paid and so forth, so as not to confuse the full amount of the advertisement with the agent ' s service costs.

Enterprise selection proposal: adjustment of judgement dimensions

In order to help you make quick decisions, we can compare the types of decision-making with the current stage of the enterprise:

It is more appropriate for an enterprise to open its own accounts:

  1. (b) The enterprises already have marketing staff familiar with Google Ads operational and policy requirements;
  2. (a) To seek to have control over the setting of accounts, advertising and data management;
  3. Availability of payment modalities and in-house operating capacity in accordance with requirements;
  4. The day-to-day advertising clearance, data analysis and optimization process is handled independently. I'm sorry.

This is a better case for seeking support from Google Advertising Agencies:

  1. (b) Enterprises lack experience with Google Ads and require professional team assistance in setting up accounts;
  2. They want to be supported by research on keywords, production of advertising material and delivery strategies.
  3. Professional staffing is required for data tracking tools such as GA4 and GTM;
  4. (b) To seek regular analysis of advertising data, a results review and recommendations for optimization;
  5. There is a clear collaborative need for account management, payment settlement and follow-up services.

When choosing the way in which an account is opened, an enterprise should not only look at the size of the advertising budget, but should also consider whether it has an independent capacity to operate in-house. If an enterprise needs only to create an account, it can open its own account or seek guidance from an agent; Further assessment of the agency ' s advertising optimization and data analysis capabilities is needed if it is to continue to access the Overseas Information Counter through Google Ads.

It's a common question of whether Google should be allowed to open an account with an agent or to register itself.

Q1: Is the ownership of the advertising account owned by the business, if the account is opened with an agent?

A: This depends on the manner in which the accounts are created and managed. Enterprises should give priority to ensuring that advertising accounts are in their own hands, that access is reserved for managers, and that it is clear whether the agent is connected through the manager ' s account or is granted other access rights. Prior to cooperation, account creation, administrator privileges and subsequent handovers should be confirmed and not rely solely on the log-in number provided by the agent.

Q2: If your own registered account is sealed, can you ask the agent to reopen it?

A: No direct reopening of accounts without an identified cause is recommended. In the event that the account is suspended for payment irregularities, advertising policy irregularities or other reasons, the specific notification from the Google Ads back office should be consulted, and the request made for modification or submission of the complaint should be made. Agents can assist in the sorting of questions and the sorting of information, but they cannot guarantee the success of complaints. Reopening should be based on Google official policy and the actual account.

Q3: Do you need an extra account or service fee for the agent to open the account?

A: Depending on the service model of the agent. Some surrogate chambers of commerce have incorporated account opening guidance into follow-up advertising cooperation services, while others provide separate account opening or account management services. Before cooperating, enterprises should confirm whether they charge account opening fees, advertising services and other fees, and also know how the billing of the billing value and the cost of the service will be settled.

Operational recommendations

The opening of Google ads is only the starting point for overseas promotion. Enterprises may choose to open their own accounts or seek agency support, depending on their team capacity. In the case of B2B foreign-trade enterprises that lack experience, it is recommended to focus on the construction of the agent ' s accounts, keyword research, advertising optimization and data analysis capacity, rather than just opening services.
