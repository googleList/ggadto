Google Ads Management FAQ

What Does Google Ads Management Include?

The proxy service includes opening accounts, the construction of a series of advertisements, the billing of advertisements, continuous optimization and weekly reports. The specific scope of implementation for search, shopping, video and Pax is determined by operational objective; The content production, site-site development and conversion tracking implementation are covered and will need to be checked separately before collaboration.

Do I Need a New Account If I Already Have One?

Not necessarily. Check the status of the account, payment settings and access rights first; Available accounts may be launched on behalf of a specific delegation of authority and should not be created over and over again to circumvent account restrictions.

How Do We Evaluate Campaign Results?

Not only the number of hits or the advertising position is compared, based on the value of the business selection valid queries, orders, cost of the take-over and transformation, combined with the number of samples, attributed to delays.

What Information Should I Provide?

Provision of websites, products or services, target areas, existing accounts, budget coverage and conversion targets. The eligibility for placement is subject to the Platform ' s policy and actual review.
