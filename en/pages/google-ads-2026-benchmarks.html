Don't judge all Campaign with the same indicator.

One of the easiest pits for Google Ads is to judge all advertising series against the same indicator. The tasks of Search, Shoping, Pax and YouTube are different, and CTR cannot be compared together.

Ask yourself: What is this Campaign responsible for? Is it a proactive search demand, a procurement drive, or is it a brand recognition? Only when the target, the market and the statistical calibration are similar can the comparison be meaningful.

CTR, CVR, CA and ROAS first.

  • CTR： The number of hits is displayed to reflect the ability of the advertisement to attract hits, which is not equal to profitability.
  • CVR： The number of conversions can be factored into the number of conversions of advertising interactions. The interaction between the different advertising formats is not necessarily a matter of clicking; Conversion actions and counting also affect results.
  • CPA： Number of cost conversions. Forms, Qualified Information Notes, and Orders are different conversions and cannot be directly compared with costs.
  • ROAS： The conversion value is three times as high as the cost of advertising. If converted value uses sales income, it is still not a profit after the cost of goods, logistics and operations.

Compared before UTM, date range, attribution settings, conversion actions and value calibres. The industry, the country, the product, the audience and the funnel phase all make significant differences.

Less than Benchmark, check first and then conclude.

I'm not going to optimise the ad right now because a number is below Benchmark. The mission, data quality and observation cycle of Campaign is checked before the issue is judged to be real.

  1. Target confirmation: sales, valid information boards or brand touch?
  2. Check tracking: is conversion double counting, is the value of the order accurate and the form valid?
  3. Selection of comparable ranges: Consider sample volume, conversion delay, promotion and seasonal change.
  4. Compare their history with their operational objectives and then use external inter-temporal support for judgement.
  5. The costs, conversion volumes, conversion values and actual business feedback before and after the adjustment are recorded to avoid a CTR-only decision on success or failure.
