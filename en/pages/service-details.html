GoogleSEO: Make the target page accept real search needs

Google rankings are optimized for Google natural search results, and Google ads are displayed for paid advertising. Both can be planned in tandem, but purchase advertising does not directly raise natural rankings. Advertising is for global operations, and keywords, technology and content implementation plans are developed in Chinese.

Google's three foundations for optimisation.

Keywords and search intent

The trade-type words are assigned to the service page by product, service, target area and client issues, and information and operational issues are assigned to the guidance page. Each page has a clear search requirement.

Technical SEO and Index

Checks the capture and indexing status, page titles, regular links, mobile experiences, site maps, inner links and duplicates to ensure that the search engine understands the pages that need to be entered.

Content & Conversion Consistency

Development of practical content around user issues, describing the scope of services, implementation processes and advisory conditions, and acceptance of valid queries through relevant page links and clear action portals.

Global-oriented, Cable-Chinese Keywords Strategy

Global markets do not amount to a single set of keywords covering all countries. First, it is clear which markets reach Chinese users, and then it combines product names, use scenes and regional planning. If English or other language is added in the future, a separate URL should be created for the full translation page, and language version relationships should be configured.

Google SEO service delivery consists of page key mapping, technical list of issues, content planning, page optimization and cycle replay. View Google ranking optimization guide Learn details of implementation.

How to evaluate Google SEO effects

View index, query word, display and click in conjunction with Search Console, split by target market and equipment; Effective advice, registration or orders resulting from natural searches are judged in the context of website analysis. The comparison should take into account branding, seasons and updates of content, and avoid looking only at the location of individual keywords.

Ranking and recording are determined by the Google system, and the optimal cycle depends on the basis of the website, competition and the quality of the content. The services are based on clear delivery and data redisposal.

How does Google Optimum and Google Advertising make a difference?

The SEOs are for nature searches, and the ads are for payment. The two can be coordinated, but the purchase of advertising does not directly raise natural rankings.

How long will the SEO be working?

The cycle is influenced by the basis of the website, competition, content and index status, and cannot give all items a fixed time or guarantee first. There should be continuous assessment of technical repairs, content delivery and effective flows.

How do you choose the key words for a global Chinese site?

First, the Chinese-language user needs in the target market are identified and pages are distributed according to service, product and client issues. If you add another language, you need a full translation of the content and a separate URL, which cannot be replaced by a language label with no corresponding page.
