It's a common problem with Google openings.

Is it the same thing about Google opening accounts and advertising audits?

Nope. The creation of accounts, the certification of advertisers, the payment of payments and the approval of advertisements are different requirements. The completion of the account registration does not mean that all advertisements can be placed, nor does it guarantee the approval of the audit.

What do you need for Google's opening?

The Google account number, actual advertising owner information, business website, contact person, payment terms and access rights are usually first identified. The authentication material is prepared on the basis of the account instructions and does not create or borrow subject information.

Can you put it on your own when you open the account?

It is possible to build a self-advertisement when account conditions permit and when the necessary configuration is completed, or to authorize a substitute. In either case, the attribution of authority and the manner in which the accounts were managed should be clearly defined.
