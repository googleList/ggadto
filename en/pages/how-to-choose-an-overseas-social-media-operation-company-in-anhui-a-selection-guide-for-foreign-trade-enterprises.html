What's Anhui's overseas media company gonna do? Guidelines for the selection of enterprises for foreign trade

A foreign-trade enterprise that used to make spare parts or sets of equipment to run overseas social media accounts with a service provider found, after having obtained several options, that the price gap was not small, but that the programme contained the words “a platform-wide coverage and a steady growth of fans”. What really needs to be recognized is: do service providers know about the target market and the procurement population? Can professional content continue to be produced? Is account operation and data reporting transparent? For B2B foreign-trade enterprises, the value of Anhui ' s overseas media operations is not just to increase fans, but more importantly to show potential buyers the business, know the product and have the opportunity to further connect. In the selection of service providers, the following aspects can be evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

Clear objectives: first, to identify the market and the procurement population

Before looking for an overseas media provider, enterprises need to define their business objectives: which countries or areas are the focus of development? Is the target client a procurement manager, an engineer or a local distributor? Would it be possible to create brand exposure, client interaction through social media, or to direct users to stand-alone sites and to submit queries?

The operational strategy will vary depending on the objective. The content for procurement managers may need to highlight product specifications, supply capacity and cooperation experience; For engineer-oriented content, technical parameters, application scenarios and solutions are described.

If the enterprise does not have a clear objective on its own, it is easy for service providers to focus on the number of posts and the growth of fans. In evaluating the programme, it is recommended that the other party's willingness to know about the enterprise's products, the target market and the client's image be assessed before it is relevant.

Channel fit: Platform selection to match the procurement population

B2B media marketing is not a platform as much as possible, but should be based on target markets, product types, customer decision-making paths, content capacity and budget options. The roles assumed by each platform could be clarified, for example, LinkedIn for professional trust-building and reaching procurement decision makers, YouTube for product presentation and technical education, Facebook and Instagram for brand presentation and remarketing, and TikTok for careful testing of the target market and content capabilities.

  • LinkedIn: A business relationship, a professional image of the business, and contacts with people in procurement, technology and management positions.
  • YouTube: It is suitable for the distribution of product presentations, equipment operations, installation processes and plant-based live videos.
  • Facebook: It can be used for business dynamics, product information dissemination and client interaction.
  • Instagram: It's good to present the look of the product, its application and its brand image through pictures and short videos.
  • TikTok: It can be used for short video content dissemination, but the suitability of industrial B2B receivers needs to be judged in relation to the target market and product characteristics.

Businesses do not have to set up all the platforms from the start. One or two core channels could be chosen before being progressively expanded in line with operational effectiveness.

When visiting service providers, two questions can be asked: Why are these platforms recommended? Are there any examples of operations in close industry or target markets? If the other side simply emphasized that the platform was “manufactured” and was unclear about the basis for the platform's selection, it would be necessary to have a better understanding of the actual operational team and content capacity.

Content planning: See if you really understand the product

Overseas media content cannot simply translate the introduction to Chinese products into English and post them in pictures. For B2B products such as accessories, mechanical equipment, the buyer is more concerned with the use of the product, the technical parameters, the application landscape, the production capacity and the security of cooperation.

Candidate service providers could provide a graphic or short video script, based on the same product and set of data, with a focus on:

  • The accuracy of product parameters and technical information;
  • (a) Whether the content focus can be drawn from the buyer ' s perspective;
  • Whether English expression is natural and in accordance with the reading habits of the target market;
  • Is there a clear interaction or direction of the content.

It would also be useful to know who provided the material, who wrote it, whether the enterprise needed to be audited and whether the pictures and videos were included in the quotations.

The frequency of the update will also be judged in relation to actual capacity. For industrial products with more sophisticated technical information, the continued release of valuable original content is more important than a day of pursuit.

Account operations: focus on day-to-day execution and authority management

The media operation is not just a regular posting, but also home page maintenance, review and private correspondence responses, fan interaction, product information updates and campaign content release.

Before signing a contract, the enterprise shall specify the following:

  • Who is responsible for the construction and daily maintenance of the home page;
  • Who handles comments, private letters and information sessions and how response times are agreed;
  • Information relating to technical parameters, certification and quotations, whether business confirmation is required;
  • Whether to include the placement of media advertisements, and how the budget for advertising and the cost of services are calculated;
  • Whether an enterprise has account number administrator authority and how to transfer content and data after cooperation.

In particular, account number privileges suggest that an enterprise register with its own subject information and retain the administrator ' s authority and do not rely entirely on the service provider ' s personal account number. While local communication and matchmaking of materials may be easier to find in the Huffy overseas media business team, it is more important to have clear responsibilities, smooth approvals and rules of engagement.

Data report: more than just fan growth

The number of fans can be used as a reference, but the B2B media performance cannot be judged separately. Businesses should also focus on content reach, interactions, website visits and practical queries.

A data report of reference value, which should contain, at a minimum:

  • Exposure data: number of content contacts, number of displays, etc.
  • Interactive data: comments, sharing, collections and private correspondence;
  • (a) Lead data: independent site access through social media links;
  • Queries data: Submission of forms from social media, private correspondence counselling and the quality of leads.

Enterprises can mark different platforms and content links through UTM parameters, then view the source of traffic and access performance in conjunction with GA4. It needs to be noted, however, that the number of social media visits does not amount to a valid query, nor does the number of private letters mean that the transaction is ultimately concluded.

When visiting the service provider, you can request access to the back-office data, content links and phase reports of the platform. The report should not only provide figures but also explain the reasons for the change in data and what adjustments are to be made in the next phase. Only reports that summarize results, do not have raw data and analytical processes have limited reference value.

Assessment of cooperation: comparison of offers with actual delivery

Differences in offers from different overseas social media operators may arise in terms of the number of platforms, original content, video production, interactive maintenance, advertising and data analysis.

When comparing prices, enterprises may wish to itemize these items and then check the scope of delivery in the contract:

  • (a) Which platforms are operational and whether they include account-building;
  • How much is provided on a monthly basis, and how the graphics and videos are organized;
  • Whether the interaction of comments, private letters and fans is included;
  • (b) Whether media advertising is provided and whether advertising costs are charged separately;
  • Regular availability of data reports and recommendations for optimization;
  • How accounts, content material and historical data are assigned.

If the service capacity is uncertain, a test run cycle and acceptance criteria could be agreed upon first. The actual delivery of inspection of quality of content, communication efficiency and data transparency will determine whether to expand the scope of the platform and cooperation without having to sign a long-term, platform-wide service from the outset.

It's a common question about how Anhui's overseas media operators choose.

Q: What should local and non-field companies choose?

A: Geography is not the only criterion for judgement. Local teams facilitate face-to-face communication and match-up of materials, but more importantly, there is no relevant industry experience, clear business processes and recognizable cases.

Q: Has the growth of overseas social media fans been slow and ineffective?

A: Not just the number of fans. It will also incorporate a combination of targeted client interactions, website visits, private correspondence consultations and effective questionnaires. The growth of fans is only one of the reference indicators.

Q: What should be the focus of comparison, given the wide gap in offers for overseas media operations?

A: Focus on actual deliverables against the number of platforms, content production, video filming, daily interactions, advertising and data reporting. The number of posts issued per month should not be compared, but the service personnel and acceptance standards should also be confirmed.

Operational recommendations

The Anhui Foreign Trade Enterprise, which chooses the overseas social media operator, may wish first to compile the target market, the purchase population, content planning, account operation and data reporting into an assessment list, and then to determine the actual capacity of the service provider in relation to its case, test content and back-office data.
