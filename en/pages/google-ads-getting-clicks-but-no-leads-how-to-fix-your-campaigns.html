How should we optimize the Google ad with any clicks or no questions?

The increasing number of Google Ads back-office hits and the normal consumption of the budget, but the website did not receive a query, which was a problem for many B2B companies when they advertised. In this case, it is not recommended that the budget be increased or advertising material be replaced at the first time. It would be more reasonable to check conversion tracking, then analyse search terms and flow quality, then sort out the pages and forms, and finally judge the quality of the trail in the context of sales feedback. Google ads have clicks or no questions, which may not necessarily be a keyword problem, or may be an inaccuracy of traffic, a lack of key information on the page, or an incorrect recording of conversion data.

Reconcile conversion tracking: confirm the accuracy of data

Before judging whether the advertisement does not contain a query, confirm whether Google Ads correctly records the conversion. If tracking settings are incomplete, back-office data may not be consistent with the queries actually received.

Common problems include only statistical contact page access and no statistical forms were submitted successfully; Third-party forms did not correctly trigger conversion events; No trace is set for telephone, mail or whatsApp contacts; Partial conversion is set to minor transformation.

Suggested focus on:

  1. The test form is actually submitted to confirm whether the conversion event has been triggered.
  2. Checks Google Ads conversion operations and main, secondary conversion settings.
  3. Compare the data from the back of the ad with the real clues in the business mailbox, website form or CRM.

If the data are not consistent, the tracking should be repaired and advertising adjusted.

Check search words: determine whether the flow is accurate

Keywords are the conditions for placement set by the main advertiser, and search words are the search content for which the user actually triggers the advertisement. They are not identical, so search term reports are an important basis for searching.

It is suggested that the following should be prioritized in order of the number of hits or the amount of consumption:

  1. (a) Broad terms not related to the product;
  2. How to, free of charge, teaching programmes, templates, low-procurement intent search terms such as recruitment;
  3. Competing words that do not fit the scope of the enterprise ' s business.

Negative keywords may be added for search terms that are clearly irrelevant; If there is a significant difference in the intended flow of advertising groups, the keywords and advertising groups can also be reclassified.

It needs to be noted that word matching is not only word-to-word, but also combines search intent and semantics. Adjustments should avoid the mistaken injury to long final words with the intent to procure.

Analysis of traffic quality: inspection of geographical areas and equipment

In addition to the search terms, it is necessary to determine whether the hits come from the target client, taking into account data such as geography, equipment, time periods and advertising networks.

For example, advertisements are directed at buyers in specific countries, but more hits come from non-target areas; Or some devices continue to generate hits without bringing in effective access and transformation.

Suggested focus analysis:

  • Geographical: concentration in target countries and distribution areas;
  • Equipment: access and transformation performance of different equipment;
  • Time period: Flow quality at different times;
  • Advertising networks: The current range of placements is in line with business objectives.

The drop should not be suspended directly on the sole basis of movement-end or night-time hits, but should be judged in the context of actual data conversion. This is also an ongoing concern in Google Ads Optimization.

Check ads and drop pages: let the page catch a click

If the advertisement highlights a particular product, authentication or application scene, but the user does not find the relevant information on the page, it may affect the conversion of the query.

The landing page will focus on three areas:

  1. It's the same information: the product and the core point of sale in the ad, if they can be found on the page.
  2. Page Matching: Product ads are as closely linked as possible to the corresponding product page and do not all point to the front page.
  3. Complete content: A clear demonstration of product parameters, application scenarios, authentication information and business service capabilities.

For B2B enterprises, product parameters, application cases and common problems are also important. The B2B landing page improvement is not just a page adjustment, but rather a help to the purchaser to determine whether the product is in line with demand and whether the supplier is worthy of contact.

Optimization of forms and trust: reduction of queries resistance

If a visitor has entered the product page and has not submitted an enquiry, the form and page trust information will need to be checked.

  • Table One: Control of the number of fields according to business needs, with priority given to the retention of necessary information such as names, business mailboxes and demand description. Checking the mobile end-to-end filling experience and providing clear feedback after submission has been successful to ensure that conversion events are properly triggered.
  • In the area of trust, it is possible to present pictures of real products, production scenes, authentication information, client cases and post-sale descriptions. Where the name of the customer and the authentication information are concerned, the information should be made available to ensure that it is true and that the necessary authorization is obtained.
  • More specific and verifiable information can help to build the trust of overseas buyers than general publicity such as “professional quality” “industry leader”.

Repeat valid query: focus on the quality of the thread

Advertising brings in forms, which does not mean that every item is a valid business opportunity. Enterprises also need to judge, in the context of sales follow-up, whether the inquiry is in line with the target market, product range and procurement needs.

Threads can be divided into categories such as valid questionnaires, invalid questionnaires, duplicate leads, etc. When conditions are ripe, valid CRM trail data can also be sent back to Google Ads, for example, through offline conversion.

At the same time, the cost of a valid query can be calculated:

Cost of valid query = cost of advertising ⁇ number of valid query

Optimization direction based on data:

  1. Search terms are not relevant: priority is given to key words and negative words;
  2. Poor quality of page access: checking for match between ads and landing pages;
  3. Pages are accessible but less submitted: optimized forms and trusted content;
  4. Queries are numerous but inefficient: they adjust the delivery strategy in the context of sales feedback.

This is also the focus of Google Ads review coverage: not only on the number of transformations, but also on the quality of the clues and the cost of getting customers.

There's a common problem with Google ads on how to optimize without a click.

Q1: Should we increase the budget first if there are any hits on Google ads or no questions?

A: No blind budget increases are recommended. Budget adjustments are considered after checking conversion tracking, search words and landing pages and confirming that there are no obvious problems with traffic and transformation pathways.

Q2: Which should be optimized first for keywords, advertisements and landing pages?

A: No fixed order. Inaccuracy of conversion data, first to track; Search for word-runs, pre-fixing keywords; Advertisements do not match pages, pre-optimal landing pages; Page access is normal, but less submitted, and then the form and trust content is checked.

Q3: How long can changes be seen after adjustment?

A: Need to be judged in relation to the number of hits, conversions and B2B procurement cycle. It is recommended that the content of each adjustment be recorded, with reasonable observation time, to avoid successive changes to multiple variables in a short period of time.

Operational recommendations

Google ads have clicks without a questionboard, often requiring a comprehensive review of traffic, pages and conversion links. The enterprise should analyse the “transformation track-search word-flow quality-fall page-form-line quality” sequence, and then determine the direction for optimization based on actual data.
