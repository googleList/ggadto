A review of the point of view: Google ads, smart bid.

In the face of Google Ads' 8.17, my first reaction was: pretty bad dad! In particular, those advertising series that have long-term budget constraints and are clearly more efficient than target-setting need to be re-understanded by the pitcher.

The new rule is simple: the system is closer to what you're filling out.

Previously, the advertising series, which had been partially budget-limited, would yield better results than the target set. This performance is taken for granted when the pitcher sees the low level of CPC and the high level of ROAS.

Raise it. Assumptions Example(a) US$ 30 for the target CPA and US$ 10 for the actual CPC in the near future; Target ROAS is 300 per cent, while the actual ROAS has recently reached 500 per cent. This is not the performance of the station, but rather the gap between the “objective” and the “real”.

According to Google, the series is now more stable to optimize the set target. Therefore, the actual results, which were better than the targets, could be closer to the completed targets; This does not mean that each conversion is strictly equal to that number.

Why do I think it's a pit?

The most uncomfortable part is... A goal that used to be easy, but that actually ran well, could now become an indicator of efficiency that needs to be taken seriously. I'm sorry. History of CPC is clearly below target and ROAS is significantly above target, and it is no longer possible to leave it behind because of “good run before”.

If the CPC target is high or the ROAS target is low, I fear that the pitcher will accept a unit economy worse than in the past at the time of setting it; In turn, too strict a target may affect the availability of flows. How the changes are to be judged in conjunction with the account data should not be seen in a single target input box.

The "suspecting" of life is a statement of concern and emotion, not the inevitable result of every account.

  • A bid is made using a relevant target value such as tCPA or tROAS.
  • in Limited by budget( subject to budgetary constraints) status.
  • In the recent past, actual CPCs were significantly below the target or actual ROAS were significantly above the target.

The types of applications listed by official FAQ include search, shopping, P Max, demand development and tourism advertising series, and demand development also relates to target CPC; Applications, video coverage and video viewing advertising series are not subject to the same change. Can history ROAS become a new target?

What would happen if the actual ROAS had recently been 300 per cent, and the target had been set directly at 300 per cent, and could not be reached at some point in time? That is what I would like to see first: historical averages do not mean that each future cycle can be repeated, and seasons, value of orders, attribution delays and flow structures change.

Instead of being repeatedly upward, downward or even suspended, I prefer to identify the cost and return of clients that business can accept, and then to look at representative historical areas. The last 30 days could be used as a starting point, but longer-cycle and fewer-sampling operations required more suitable observation windows.

One or two days of fluctuations should not be attributed directly to the new rules, nor should the set-up be constantly modified to “collect historical values”.

Budget constraints do not amount to a budget increase

I'm worried that the pitcher is gradually accepting higher CAs or lower ROAS in order to keep the volume. It is called the “inducing relaxation standard”, which is my view of the platform's incentives, not an official expression of its own purpose.

An increase in the budget could be an operational option, but not the only one. The Government has also given options for retaining targets, adjusting targets to take into account recent performance, self-definition of objectives to meet operational requirements or adjustment strategies. Google will not automatically increase your budget or change your targets.

I will not add money simply by a “budget-restricted” hint. Whether the increase is made depends first on the value of the marginal order or information disk and whether the team can afford additional inputs; The target value is also not a guarantee of profit, conversion volume or actual ROI.

What should the pitcher do: screen, record, adjust?

  1. _Other Organiser Priority is given to checking budget constraints and the series of target value bids to check whether there has been a clear advantage over the target.
  2. Establishment of baselines: The most recent representational target, CAS/ROAS, actual value, cost, conversion volume and conversion value is recorded, while the attribution factor is delayed.
  3. Reconciliation of operational quality: The profit of actual orders, a valid enquiry board and a final deal are more important than a single platform indicator.
  4. Gradual adjustments: If the objectives are indeed inconsistent with current operational requirements, they are adjusted in phases and, to the extent possible, do not change objectives, budgets, materials and major transformations at the same time.
  5. Time for observation: Comparison of results in conjunction with the conversion cycle; Time, cause and expectation for each change.

My recommendation is to implement it prudently, and not to fix all accounts to lower or enhance the CPC. If the target is too radical, the sample is inadequate or the error is tracked, a copying of someone else ' s number may also be problematic.

As for the budget increase, I choose to wait and see.

Google gives proposals for bid mechanisms and optimization, rather than assuring each advertiser that “a budget increase will preserve past performance”. I would have preferred to check targets, track them and actual operational feedback for the previously well-performing series.

My attitude is clear: Read the settings and data, and decide whether to expand. The article is a commentary on changes and a philosophy of implementation, and the specific adjustments still need to be combined with the account situation.
