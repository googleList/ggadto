Two acquisition channels, with separate plans and measures

01 · ChatGPT Ads Management

Paid advertising support starts with account eligibility, target-market availability and industry requirements. Then confirm budget, creative, landing pages and measurement. Launch depends on the platform’s current availability and approval.

02 · GEO / AI SEO

Improve the conditions that help generative search discover, understand and cite your website. Work on accurate product information, customer questions, content structure and technical accessibility. Buying ads does not buy organic citations.

What the work covers

Business and Search Intent Review

Understand your products, customers, markets and languages. Map brand, comparison, solution and purchase-intent questions to appropriate pages.

ChatGPT Campaign Preparation

Assess available accounts and markets. Plan ad copy, image requirements, landing pages and measurement. Build and optimize within the platform’s available features after confirming access, budget and approval requirements.

GEO Content and Page Improvements

Review product, service, comparison and FAQ pages. Answer the main question first, then add verifiable specifications, terms and evidence. Avoid keyword stuffing and unsupported endorsements.

Technical SEO and Structured Data

Review crawling, indexing, canonical URLs, language versions, internal links and page speed. Structured data must reflect visible page content; adding schema is not a guarantee of AI citations.

Landing Pages and Inquiry Handling

Align ad copy, content and service descriptions. Make the inquiry path, suitable customers and next step clear. Confirm whether forms, tracking implementation and landing-page development are included. A click is not a qualified inquiry.

Reporting and Next Steps

For paid campaigns, review spend, clicks, conversions and qualified inquiries. For GEO, record a consistent question set, test dates, cited pages and search performance. State the sample and context; one AI answer is not a stable ranking.

ChatGPT Ads and GEO Questions

Is GEO the same as buying ChatGPT ads?

No. ChatGPT ads are paid placements. GEO improves the discoverability and citation readiness of content in generative search. Purchasing ads does not give an advertiser control over ChatGPT’s organic answers.

Can you guarantee AI recommendations or first-page rankings?

No. Platforms determine citations, visibility and rankings, which vary by question, market, time and system. We review completed work, verifiable content and actual data rather than promise fixed placements, rankings or inquiry volumes.

Can every country and industry advertise on ChatGPT?

Eligibility must be assessed first. Accounts, markets, industries and creative must meet the platform’s current availability and policies. If requirements are not met, prepare website content and landing pages first; we do not promise to bypass approval.

What do you need to get started?

Your website, product or service details, target countries, customer profile, budget range, existing ad accounts and measurement tools. Grant only the necessary account permissions; do not submit passwords or verification codes through inquiry forms.

What if we already have SEO and Google Ads?

Start with a gap review. Keep useful content and existing technical foundations. Add work only where target questions, paid channels or landing-page journeys require it, rather than rebuilding unnecessarily.
