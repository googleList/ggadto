Google Advertising Self-Investigated Tuven Academy

To launch Google Ads on its own, it can start with business objectives, conversion tracking and a clear search ad. This document is supplemented by 13 screenshots, with B2B query as the main example and how the electrician or other target needs to be judged.

Let's get to the screenshots first.

The screenshot is in the order in which it was actually carried out, and the original red comments are retained. If the text is supplemented by an official note available in October 2026, please refer to the current setting when the interface field is different; Each option is judged by the relationship between objectives, transformation and budget.

Step 01

Google Ads, create a new advertising series

Confirms that Google Ads accounts can be accessed, settled and configured and then create a new advertising series from the " Create " or add-on entry on the page. The screenshot shows the operating path of the existing account; When new users enter for the first time, they may first see the guide to business information and the first advertisement.

The product or service description, the land page, the target market, the test budget and the contact details are prepared prior to delivery. The questionnaire, purchase or registration process on the website should be properly completed. Not yet opened, read first Google open-account information and process guide.

Official Google: Create a search and advertising series

Step 02

First, read the relationship between the advertising series, the advertising group and the advertising.

Markets, budgets and targets are planned at the level of the advertising series, keywords and advertisements are organized at the level of the advertising group, and sales points and action guides are written at the level of advertising. An advertising series may include multiple advertising groups, but each group needs to be around a similar demand for products or services.

For example, packaging suppliers can break up the advertising group for products such as “constructive packaging” and each corresponding keywords, scripts and landing pages. Where there are significant differences in the national budget, operational time or the gross product, consideration may be given to dividing the advertising series to facilitate the judgement of inputs.

Step 03

B2B's Query Target. The electrician's around the purchase target.

B2B projects usually focus on potential clients and effective queries; Electrician projects usually focus on sales, orders and revenues. Before selecting a target, write down the action that the client will eventually complete, so as not to follow the selection simply because it is recommended by the interface.

“Web traffic” is appropriate for the time-bound testing of access, but the increase in hits is not equal to the increase in orders. Target selection is also not a substitute for event configuration: the actual conversion action and landing page acceptance are checked, whether the sales or potential customers are selected.

Official Google: Create a search and advertising series

Step 04

Set real business actions to transformation

The screenshot contains targets such as “post-click” “presenting a potential customer form”. For B2B projects, the successful submission of an information disk, completion of an appointment or business-defined consultation is usually closer to the target than the regular page view. Checks if the default conversion target is mixed with an interactive action unrelated to this item.

Clicking on the WhatsApp button only proves that the user clicks on the portal, does not directly prove that the user initiated the dialogue, much less that the query is valid. Such hits can be used as a supporting indicator, then combined with actual communication or operational business back-to-back judgement of quality.

If GA4 is used, the event can be activated and the key event is established as Google Ads, checking the main/secondary transformation and the target for use in the advertising series. A real test is done before you go online to check the double count and the non-trigger event.

Google Official: Create an ad for the GA4 event

Step 05

Select the ads on demand, not the Pax budget alone.

The main presentation here is Google Search Advertisements: When a target customer will proactively search for a product or service, the demand can be carried out using keywords, and the search words, files and landing pages can be more easily reset.

PROMs cover multiple channels and are selected with regard to transformation quality, materials, business objectives and account conditions. The choice of “Pax on budget is not a common conclusion. When advertising for purchases is required, it is also necessary to confirm the commodity data and the Mercant Center conditions; Searches, displays, videos and Pax should not use the same set of test expectations.

I'd like to compare the various types of ads. Google Advertisement Type and Policy Guide.

Official Google: Create a search and advertising series

Step 06

Confirm the way you get the invitees and get ready to take over the page.

Site visits, call calls, potential client forms or other available means are selected on the basis of the project. B2B selects the relevant target for the form, distinguishing between the form on its own website and the Google Native Spectrum Form; The two are set, applied under different conditions and in a way that allows data to be exchanged.

The landing page recommends a clear demonstration of products, applicable scenes, delivery capacity and communication methods to reduce the number of unrelated jumps. The points of sale proposed in the advertisement should be found on the page and the form fields should correspond to the request for a request for information.

Step 07

Use of uniform naming rules to facilitate subsequent management

The name does not directly determine the effect of the advertisement, but a clear name can reduce later misreading data. The recommendations include target markets, products, types of advertising and targets, such as: US-Packaging-Search-Leads-202610.

The same team will first harmonize naming habits, then create a group of advertisements and a version of the material. The material changes and budget adjustments can record the date and cause, and facilitate a review of the implementation records when the effects change.

Step 08

Select the bid policy and check the new client's acquisition conditions

When looking at the number of questionnaires, strategies to target the number of conversions can be evaluated; Where there are significant differences in the different conversion values, the value should be accurately recorded and then the conversion value should be considered as the target. Targets CPA or ROAS should be set in combination with cost, profit and real transformation data, and it is not advisable to fill in a very low target at will.

The number of transformations + the cost of each conversion of the optional target in the screenshot is presented as an interface. Google has adjusted some of the search smart bid names since June 2026. The names that may be seen in different accounts may differ and should be judged by strategic behaviour and current tips.

“Only for new customers” is an optional business strategy and is not a mandatory option for new accounts. Whether or not to activate depends on the target, customer recognition and corresponding qualifications; The “1,000 active users”, referred to in the screenshot, refers in the relevant official requirements to the audience membership conditions of a particular network, which cannot simply be understood as the cumulative number of 1,000 visitors to the website.

Google Official: Conversion value bid and target setting · Official Google: new clients acquiring and qualifying their audiences

Step 09

Match the drop range to the target market.

The first test allows for a clear search range of networks, with additional traffic, such as the search of partners, assessed separately, rather than accepting all recommended options without discrimination. (a) The geographical location is chosen by the country or region where the services are actually available for sale or service; “Globally oriented” does not mean that each advertising series requires the choice of all countries.

An advanced view of geographic location identifies those who want to cover the target area or who show interest in the area. Cross-border electricians and service lines should be judged on the basis of distribution, the scope of services and the quality of the actual queries.

Language options in the screenshot may have changed

According to Google, the search for advertisements began in September 2026 to adjust the language matching, the language set in the advertising series was gradually removed and the search for advertisements was matched by the language in which the advertisement was made. If the current interface still displays language options, process them by the account hint; Whether or not a field exists, keywords, ads and page language should be used to match the target client.

Official Google: Language orientation and 2026 restructuring

Step 10

Distinguishing "positioning" from "observation" to avoid unintentional reduction of flow

In the search advertising, “observation” is used to view the performance of a designated audience, without additional restrictions on coverage; “Loving” would allow advertising to cover only those who meet the requirements of the audience. They are not the same function.

If the integrity of the audience list has not yet been confirmed, performance can be observed before a decision is made on the basis of actual data. The word “precision” should not be used to tick the place, otherwise it might exclude those who need it but do not choose a target audience. The audience set-up still needs to be replicated with keywords and actual operational objectives.

Official Google: Searching for audience location and observation

Step 11

Let each set of keywords correspond to a product and a landing page

Filling in your own landing page or product information allows you to obtain suggestions for keywords, but the list of recommendations still needs to be filtered. Prefer to words that are relevant to the needs of the target client and that can be taken over on the current page, rather than placing the entire industry in the same advertising group.

For example, customized packaging operations can organize keywords around specific products, uses and procurement intentions, and then consider matching. Prepares to deny keywords and to update them on the basis of actual search terms when they are online; Searches and keywords are two types of data, and the full flow cannot be judged by a keyword alone.

The accounts offer different matching and automation options. The manner in which the tests are conducted is documented, and adjustments are needed in conjunction with budget, tracking quality and search terms.

Step 12

Fill in the ad file, preview and set an average daily budget

The final arrival should point to the corresponding product or service page. The title and description are the respective product, advantages, credible information and action guidance, e.g. “See product specifications” “Access customized offers”; Don't put unproven promises in advertising.

Self-adapted search fairs combine different titles and descriptions, and individual articles or combinations should be established. View mobile phones and desktop previews, check web sites, spelling, content consistency and required material. Advertising effectiveness is an indicator of material feedback, not a guarantee of transformation.

The budget is an “average daily budget” and is not a fixed ceiling that is never exceeded on a daily basis. For most advertising series, Google ' s daily and monthly spending limits are twice and 30.4 times the average daily budget, respectively; The application of exceptions and the rules following the budgetary changes are subject to the accounts and official statements. The actual costs are checked on a regular basis, after setting up the available test input.

Review of conversions, regions, networks, bids, budgets, landing pages and settlements before submission. Advertisements are still subject to review after they have been submitted and when restrictions arise, they are dealt with on a specific basis.

Official Google: Self-adapted search ads · Official Google: average daily budget

Step 13

Use GA4 and Google Ads to redisclose the results after they go online.

The screenshot shows active users, number of events, new users and key events, which can help to understand the GA4 interface. It is not a complete alternative to Google Ads ' advertising statements, nor can it be judged on the basis of a percentage increase in the cost or profitability of the clients.

Check date, source/media, advertising series and event calibration, and then combine Google Ads with Google Ads clicks, costs, conversions and a valid business side-by-side query. The conversion of GA4 key events and the bid for Google Ads needs to be properly configured; Different attributions and statistical rules may lead to differences in data between the two platforms.

  • Flows are not relevant: check search words, areas, networks and negative words.
  • Multiple clicks, few queries: check landing pages, event triggers and advisory acceptances.
  • Numerous but poor-quality information sheets: check key transformation definitions and operational feedback.
  • The change in costs is evident: reconciliation of budgets, strategies, tracking and recent changes in records.

Each adjustment is made to record the reasons and allows for a time of observation that matches the business transformation cycle. Do not change all settings at the same time so that it is not possible to judge which change works.

Official Google: GA4 Critical Events · Google Official: Create an ad for the GA4 event

Final check before release

  • Operational objectives, major transformations and budgets correspond to each other.
  • The landing page is accessible, and the form is submitted on a mobile basis.
  • Keywords, scripts, audiences are consistent with the target market.
  • The conversion events were tested and the costs and payment modalities were confirmed.
  • Checking data and feedback arrangements for queries after being clearly online.
