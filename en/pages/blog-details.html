How can Google rank be optimized: a guide to the SEO on the global Chinese site?

  • Home
  • >
  • Google ranking optimization guide

Let's start with Google rankings and Google ads.

Google rankings usually refer to the location of the natural search results; Google ads are paid for by Google Ads. Advertising allows for rapid testing of search needs, and SEO builds natural visibility through website technology and content. The budgets, data and effects of the two should be assessed separately.

Step 1: Targeting markets and keywords

Description of products, clients and target areas, collection of branding, service, product and problem words. (c) To refer Google Service Query to the Advertisement Page and to the Guide page on issues such as what Google needs to open an account, so as to avoid multiple pages that simultaneously rob the same subject.

Step 2: Checking capture and indexing

Check the domain name, submit site maps and check important URLs in Google Seach Console after you go online. Checks HTTP status, robots rules, canonical, moving-end content and internal links. Site maps help to find pages, but cannot be guaranteed.

Step 3: Let the content answer specific questions

The title, main title and description should contain an accurate summary of the page. The text describes needs, services, processes and judgement conditions, with a meaningful internal chain and picture description. Do not insert all keywords over and over again on each page; GoogleSEO should give priority to helping users to make their decisions.

Step 4: Planning content for global Chinese users

Check product availability, business time and advisory succession by target country and region. The Chinese language content is suitable for Chinese search needs. When additional languages are added, full translation pages should be provided, avoiding only translation of titles.

Step five: Rewinding with real business data on a continuous basis

Observation of non-brand queries, page displays, hits and natural flows, combined with effective queries or order quality. The fluctuations in rankings need to be judged in relation to competition, seasons and index status, and the full effects cannot be evaluated on the basis of a single search screenshot.
